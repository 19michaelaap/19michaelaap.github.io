Travel to Klagenfurt
Klagenfurt is located on multiple major international train routes. There are also multiple Flix bus routes which stop in Klagenfurt.
For those who will be flying in, there are three major international airports close to Klagenfurt:
  • Vienna Airport (VIE)
  • Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE)
  • Munich Airport (MUC)
There are also a number of smaller airports which are well-connected to Klagenfurt:
  • Graz Airport (GRZ)
  • Salzburg Airport (SZG)
  • Ljubljana Airport (LJU)
  • Trieste Airport (TRS)
  • Bratislava Airport (BTS)
  • Treviso Airport (TSF)
Specific information for travel from these airports to Klagenfurt will be provided closer to the conference dates.
